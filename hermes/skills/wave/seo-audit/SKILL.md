for fixes. Markets: UK, Netherlands, Singapore, Thailand, Cambodia, plus Mediterranean/Caribbean
yachting hubs.

2. **Technical**: crawl key pages: titles, meta, headings, speed (Core Web Vitals), mobile, schema
   (Organization, LocalBusiness per office, Product), sitemap/robots, hreflang for languages.
3. **Content gaps**: keywords competitors rank for and Wave doesn't (e.g. "superyacht Starlink
   installation", "yacht Wi-Fi upgrade", "marine AV integrator Thailand").
4. **Plan**: 10 actions ranked by impact/effort; 4 article briefs; local listings per office.
