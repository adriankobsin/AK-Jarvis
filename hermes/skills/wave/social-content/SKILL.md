# Social content

Brand: premium, technical, cinematic; see `search_knowledge` "brand" and "social media". Never post
client yachts, names or locations without written permission (superyacht privacy).

1. **Plan**: a weekly calendar: 3–5 posts mixing project showcases (anonymised), tech tips
   (Starlink, Wi-Fi, AV), behind-the-scenes, product news (anchor-pocket camera, DBoard Designer), and
   recruitment or events. Tie to SEO keywords (see seo-audit skill).
2. **Create**: per platform: caption (LinkedIn longer and professional; Instagram short, with
   hashtags), alt text, image (built-in image generation or photos from SharePoint), optional 15–30 s
   video script. Keep a consistent look.
3. **Review**: show Adrian the batch as one message (or on the core screen). Optional
   `consult_gpt` check for tone and claims.
4. **Publish**: after approval, schedule with `mcp_social_*` at the best local times for the audience
   (Europe mornings for yacht management; Asia evenings for venues).
5. **Report**: weekly: reach, engagement, clicks, followers; what worked; next week's ideas.
